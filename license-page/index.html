Questions

Frequently asked

Is it really unlimited?

Yes. Every plan lets you print and sell as many MYTY Mini cases and add-on modules as you want while your license is active. The only difference between plans is how long they last and the extras that come with them.

What happens when my license ends?

Your right to sell new prints ends, and you stop receiving new revisions. Cases you've already printed can still be sold for 30 days. Renew before the end date to keep selling without a gap.

Can I switch to a longer plan?

Yes. Choose a longer plan when you renew, or contact us to upgrade mid-term and we'll credit the time you have left.

Why does every order need a verification card?

The card proves to your buyer that they've bought a genuine, licensed MYTY Mini. When they register it, the sale counts toward your verified-sales total in the directory, and they get 15% off official add-ons. Putting the card in the box is the only thing you need to do.

What materials should I use?

Use heat-resistant materials such as PETG, ASA or better, and follow the minimum print settings in the print guide. PLA isn't approved for structural parts.

Who is responsible for the products I sell?

You are the manufacturer of the cases you print, so product safety and consumer-law obligations in the countries you sell to are yours to meet.

Can I share my files with a friend or my team?

No. Licenses are personal and the files are made for your license alone. Each maker needs their own license.
